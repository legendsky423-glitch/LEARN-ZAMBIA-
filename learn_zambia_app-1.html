FORM 1 • HISTORY
1.1 Introduction to History

Lesson 1: Reasons for Learning History

What is History?

History is the study of past events and human experiences. It helps us understand what happened, how people lived, and how societies changed over time.

Remember: History is not only about memorising dates. It also involves asking questions, examining evidence, and understanding change.

Why do we learn History?

  • Learn from past experiences: Past successes and mistakes can help people make thoughtful decisions today.
  • Understand our families and communities: Stories, photographs, records, and memories can show how a community developed.
  • Become responsible citizens: Studying the past can help us appreciate cultural heritage and other people’s experiences.
  • Develop critical thinking: We learn to ask who provided information and what evidence supports it.
  • Understand other fields: History can help explain developments in areas such as medicine, music, and law.

Example from everyday life

An older relative might describe how your neighbourhood looked when they were young. Comparing that account with photographs or other records can help you understand what has changed.

Key words

  • Past: time that has already happened.
  • Event: something that happens.
  • Evidence: information used to support an account or conclusion.
  • Heritage: traditions, places, and things passed down from earlier generations.

Activity

Ask an older relative or community member what your area was like when they were younger. Write down two changes they remember and one thing that stayed the same.
